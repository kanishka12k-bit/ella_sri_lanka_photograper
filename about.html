Meet the Artist

I Capture Moments That
Last a Lifetime

Hello — I'm Champika Rathnayaka, a fine-art wedding and portrait photographer based between Ella and the world's most breathtaking landscapes. With over a decade behind the lens, I've come to believe that the best photographs don't interrupt a moment — they grow out of it.

My work is rooted in documentary storytelling, shaped by a love of light, texture, and the quiet in-between seconds that reveal who people truly are. Whether it's the way a bride laughs at her own nerves, or two people sharing a glance that needs no words — those are the frames I live for.

"Photography is the art of making the invisible — feeling, joy, love — visible."

— Ella - Sri Lanka

Luxury photography experiences for travelers, coupless, and families visiting Ella, Sri Lanka.
