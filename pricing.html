Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you travel for destination shoots?

Absolutely. I'm based in Bandarawela, Sri Lanka but available worldwide. Travel fees apply for shoots outside a 60 km radius — contact me for a custom quote.

How do I secure my booking date?

A 30% retainer and a signed agreement lock your date. The remaining balance is due one week before the shoot day.

When will I receive my photos?

Standard delivery is within 14 days. The Signature package includes priority 7-day delivery. A sneak-peek gallery of select images is sent within 48 hours.

Can I customise a package?

Yes! Every couples's needs are different. Reach out through the contact form and we'll build a bespoke package that suits your vision perfectly.
