12 Best Photography Spots in Ella, Sri Lanka

A Local Photographer's Guide

Best Photography Spots in Ella, Sri Lanka

Ella, Sri Lanka, is one of the most beautiful destinations on the island for photography. Surrounded by lush green mountains, tea plantations, waterfalls, railway tracks and spectacular viewpoints, Ella offers incredible locations for couple photoshoots, honeymoon photography, pre-wedding sessions, surprise proposals, solo portraits, family photography and travel photography.

From the iconic Nine Arch Bridge and breathtaking Little Adam's Peak to scenic tea plantations, the Nanu Oya–Ella train journey, Ella Swing, waterfalls, hidden viewpoints and romantic proposal locations — explore every spot below and start planning your Ella photography experience. 📸

Spot 01

Nine Arch Bridge — Ella's Most Iconic Photo Spot

The Nine Arch Bridge is one of the most famous landmarks in Ella and one of the best photography spots in Ella, Sri Lanka.

Surrounded by lush green jungle and mountains, this historic railway bridge creates the classic Ella travel photograph. The location becomes even more spectacular when a train passes across the bridge.

For couples and honeymooners, the bridge provides a beautiful combination of architecture, nature, and Sri Lanka’s famous railway scenery.

Best for: Couples · Solo · Family Best time: Morning / Evening
Spot 02

Little Adam's Peak — Sunrise & Sunset Photography

Little Adam’s Peak is one of the most popular viewpoints in Ella and one of the best places for couple photography and surprise proposals.

The walk to the viewpoint is relatively accessible, and once you reach the top, you are rewarded with spectacular views across Ella’s green mountains and valleys.

The location is especially beautiful around sunrise and sunset when the mountains are covered in soft, warm light.

Best for: Couples · Proposals · Solo Best time: Sunrise / Sunset
Spot 03

Ella Rock — Adventure & Dramatic Mountain Photography

If you want something more adventurous, Ella Rock is one of the best photography locations around Ella.

The hike is more challenging than Little Adam’s Peak, but the dramatic mountain views make it worthwhile.

From the top, you can capture wide landscape photographs as well as adventurous portraits.

Best for: Adventure · Couples · Solo Best time: Sunrise / Morning
Spot 04

Nanu Oya ↔ Ella Train Journey — A Cinematic Experience

The Nanu Oya to Ella train journey is one of the most scenic railway experiences in Sri Lanka.

The route travels through the hill country, passing tea plantations, mountains, forests, tunnels, railway bridges, and misty valleys.

For photography, this journey is perfect for natural, candid and cinematic images.

Best for: Couples · Solo · Travellers Best time: Morning / Daytime
Spot 05

Ella Tea Plantations — Green, Natural & Romantic

Ella is surrounded by beautiful tea-growing areas, making the tea plantations in Ella one of the best locations for natural photography.

The endless rows of green tea plants combined with mountain scenery create an elegant Sri Lankan backdrop.

This location works particularly well for honeymoon and pre-wedding photography.

Best for: Couples · Solo · Pre‑Wedding Best time: Morning / Evening
Spot 06

Ravana Falls — Nature & Waterfall Photography

Ravana Falls is one of the most popular natural attractions near Ella.

The waterfall, rocks and surrounding tropical greenery create a completely different photography environment compared with the mountain viewpoints.

Best for: Nature · Couples · Travel Best time: Daytime
Spot 07

Ella Swing — Fun & Scenic Travel Photography

The Ella Swing is a fun photography experience for travellers who want something different from traditional mountain viewpoints.

With the mountains and greenery in the background, the swing can create beautiful and playful photographs.

It is especially popular for couples and solo travellers looking for memorable travel photographs.

Best for: Couples · Solo · Fun Travel Photos Best time: Morning / Evening
Spot 08

Mahamevnawa Buddhist Monastery — Culture & Architecture

If you want to include something cultural in your Ella photography experience, Mahamevnawa Buddhist Monastery offers a peaceful setting.

The architecture, Buddhist surroundings and scenic environment create a different visual style from the typical mountain and railway photographs.

Best for: Architecture · Culture · Solo · Couples Best time: Daytime
Spot 09

Secret Proposal Locations in Ella

Ella is an incredible destination for a surprise marriage proposal.

While famous viewpoints such as Little Adam’s Peak are beautiful, a quieter and more private location can sometimes create a much more intimate experience.

Best for: Surprise Proposals · Couples Best time: Sunset / Evening
Spot 10

Haputale & Lipton's Seat

If you have additional time during your Sri Lanka trip, travelling from Ella towards Haputale and Lipton’s Seat can provide spectacular landscape photography opportunities.

The area is famous for its tea estates, misty mountains and panoramic views over the surrounding countryside.

Best for: Couples · Solo · Landscapes Best time: Sunrise / Morning
Spot 11

Secret Waterfall — A Hidden Natural Experience

If you prefer quieter locations away from busy tourist attractions, a Secret Waterfall can be a great option.

Surrounded by greenery, rocks and flowing water, this type of location creates a more natural and adventurous photography experience.

Best for: Couples · Solo · Nature Best time: Morning / Daytime
Spot 12

Secret & Hidden Viewpoints in Ella

If you want photographs that feel more private and less like typical tourist photographs, consider exploring some of the quieter viewpoints around Ella.

These locations can be ideal for couples and honeymooners who want natural photographs without large groups of tourists in the background.

Best for: Couples · Honeymoon · Solo Best time: Morning / Evening

Before You Go

Tips for Getting the Best Photos in Ella

Don't choose a location just because it's famous. Think about whether you want mountains, tea plantations, trains, waterfalls, culture or romantic sunset photographs.

The same location can look completely different in morning and evening light — this guide's time suggestions above are a great starting point.

Light, neutral colours work beautifully against Ella's green landscapes, while elegant outfits create a more sophisticated pre-wedding look.

Some locations are close to Ella town, while others require hiking or driving. Avoid planning too many locations without enough time between them.

A local photographer can help with posing, composition, lighting, location selection and timing so you don't have to worry about what to do in front of the camera.
