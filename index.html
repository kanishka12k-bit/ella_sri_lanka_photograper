About

I’m Champika Rathnayaka (Champi), the photographer behind Ella Sri Lanka Photographer.

Discovering Ella Through My Lens

  • From hidden scenic locations to breathtaking sunrise viewpoints and peaceful tea estates, I love helping travelers discover the most beautiful sides of Ella while creating unforgettable memories through photography.

My Photography Style & Expertise

  • Since 2019, I have specialized in proposal, couples, pre-wedding, solo, family, and travel photography for visitors exploring Sri Lanka. My photography style focuses on natural emotions, real moments, and cinematic storytelling in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere.

More Than Just Photos

  • More than simply taking photos, my goal is to help people connect beautiful memories with the incredible landscapes of Ella. Seeing clients happy and creating experiences they will always remember brings me genuine joy.

Trusted by Travelers

  • Many of my clients come through recommendations from previous travelers, which means a lot to me and motivates me to continue creating meaningful experiences for every couples and visitor I work with.
